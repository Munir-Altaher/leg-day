# Assembly

Files: `cad/parts/hip_module.SLDASM` (2-DOF, stands alone: the fallback hand-in) nested in `cad/parts/leg_3dof.SLDASM` (+ ab/ad motor + body bracket + rig plate). Both are generated by `cad/build_assembly.py` with every part in its home pose (q = 0, leg straight down). The assembly origin is the hip-pitch / ab-ad axis intersection. SolidWorks interference detection at the home pose: **0 interferences** in both assemblies (`cad/parts/interference_home_pose.txt`).

> Note: because of an API workaround, each part's own origin sits at (−300, −300, −300) mm (see `ASSUMPTIONS.md`). The parts' Front/Top/Right planes are all parallel to the body frame, so the angle mates below work as written.

## Mates (generated by `cad/build_assembly.py`; drag the thigh, shank or hip module to move the joints)
All parts share one frame, so rigid groups are held by three coincident base-plane mates, and each joint is a named reference axis in the parts (`HIP_AXIS`, `KNEE_AXIS`, `ABAD_AXIS`).

**hip_module.SLDASM**
1. Fixed: `module_plate_inboard`, `module_flange_back`, `module_flange_front`, `module_frame_outboard`, `ref_motor_hip`, `ref_motor_knee` (bolted together).
2. **Hip:** `HIP_axis` (thigh_outer HIP_AXIS ↔ plate HIP_AXIS, coincident) + `HIP_plane` (Front Planes, coincident) + `HIP_limit` (limit angle, Right ↔ Right, 0–100°).
3. `thigh_cover` rigid to `thigh_outer` (3 plane coincidences).
4. **Knee-motor output:** `hip_shaft_adapter` on HIP_AXIS + Front Planes (spins freely). `ref_pulley_20T_kit` rigid to it.
5. **Knee:** `KNEE_axis` (shank ↔ thigh_outer KNEE_AXIS) + `KNEE_plane` + `KNEE_limit` (Right ↔ Right, 0–150°).
6. `knee_pulley_60T` rigid to `shank`. **Ankle:** `snowshoe` on `ANKLE_AXIS` + Front planes + limit angle (snowshoe Top ↔ shank Right 38–79°, i.e. shank angle 11–52° in the pad frame).

**leg_3dof.SLDASM**
1. Fixed: `body_spine`, `body_bracket_back`, `body_bracket_front`, `rig_plate`, `ref_motor_abad`.
2. `hip_module` is a **flexible** sub-assembly (unfixed), so the hip and knee also drag inside the full leg.
3. **Ab/ad:** `ABAD_axis` (module_plate_inboard ABAD_AXIS ↔ body_spine ABAD_AXIS) + `ABAD_plane` (Right Planes) + `ABAD_limit` (Top ↔ Top, 0–45°).

**Checked by `cad/verify_mates.py`** (results in `cad/parts/mate_check.txt`): no mate errors after rebuild.
- Each joint moves when driven inside its range: hip to 60°, knee to 90°, ab/ad to 30°.
- Each limit stops the joint when it's driven past it: hip 125°, knee 170°, ab/ad 70°.

Limitation: a SolidWorks angle mate between two planes is unsigned (0–180°). So the hip limit acts as |q1| ≤ 100° and the ab/ad limit as |q0| ≤ 45°, which matches the design. The knee CAD limit is 0–150°, so in CAD the knee can be dragged to a small negative angle; firmware enforces 15–150°. The belt coupling isn't mated: the hip-shaft adapter turns independently of the knee pulley. Add a gear mate (20 : 60) between the two pulley cylinders to animate it.

## Build order (every screw head reachable)
1. **Print the fit coupon first**, update `FIT_*` in `params.py`, regenerate. Print everything else with **no supports** (orientations in `PRINT_SETTINGS.md`).
2. **Heat-set inserts (16 × M3).** Iron them in before assembling anything:
   - 4 in the thickened zones of `module_plate_inboard` (motor-side face);
   - 4 in the top of the spine's back block;
   - 4 in the top of the spine's front end;
   - 4 in the underside of the spine.
3. **Body bracket.**
   - Bolt the ab/ad motor's back face to `body_bracket_back` (5 × M2.5 × 8, from behind).
   - Press a 625ZZ into `body_bracket_front` (from the inner side, against the lip).
   - Screw each wall's foot **down** into the spine inserts (4 × M3 × 12 per wall, with washers). Nothing needs drilling.
   - Bolt `rig_plate` under the spine (4 × M3 × 10, heads in the counterbores).
4. **Module flanges.**
   - Screw `module_flange_back` and `module_flange_front` onto `module_plate_inboard` (2 × M3 × 12 each through the flange feet into the plate inserts, with washers).
   - Bolt the back flange to the ab/ad motor output (3 × M2.5 × 8 from inside). The motor's Ø15 boss enters the through-hole.
   - Do this **before** the knee motor goes in, or the screw heads are unreachable.
   - Push the M5 stub-axle bolt (head in the front flange's hex pocket) out through the front flange.
5. **Knee motor + C-frame.** Lay the C-frame's tab plate on the inboard plate's inboard face. Fix the knee motor's back face through both with 5 × M2.5 × 12 (3 mm into the motor's 5 mm holes). The C-frame is now located by five screws on a Ø45 circle instead of 2 self-tappers.
6. **Hip pulley shaft:**
   - Put an **M5 × 16** head into the `hip_shaft_adapter` hex pocket. It was M5 × 25, which reached about 4 mm into the hip motor's face.
   - Bolt the adapter to the knee motor output (3 × M2.5 × 6 in counterbores: 2.5 mm engagement in the 3 mm holes).
7. **Thigh.**
   - Press a 625 into `thigh_cover` at the hip and another at the knee (from the channel side), and a 625 into `thigh_outer` at the knee.
   - Bolt `thigh_outer` to the hip motor output (3 × M2.5 × 8 + one M2.5 washer each = 2.5 mm engagement; heads inside the channel). The motor's Ø15 boss enters the through-hole.
8. **Knee.**
   - Put the M5 × 25 head in the shank's hex pocket and slide on one M5 washer (it spaces the shank off the bearing's inner race).
   - Pass the bolt through the `thigh_outer` knee bearing, a **0.5 mm M5 shim washer**, and `knee_pulley_60T`. The shim replaces the pulley's second hub spacer, which was removed so the pulley prints flat.
   - Thread the M5 nylock into the pulley's hex pocket with medium threadlocker.
9. **Belt.**
   - Cut 372 mm (342 mm loop = 171 teeth, + 2 × 15 mm clamp overlap).
   - Loop it over the 60T pulley, slide the kit 20T pulley onto the M5 shaft from step 6 inside the loop, and close it with the kit clamp. The clamp must sit on a span, never on a pulley, over the full range.
   - Put the idler (M3 bolt) in the slot loose.
10. **Close the thigh:** offer up `thigh_cover` (its hip bearing slides over the hip shaft and the knee bolt end goes into the knee bearing). Screw on 3 × M3 × 16 at the hip bosses and 4 × M3 × 20 at the ears.
11. **Hang the hip/knee stack in the module.** Bolt the hip motor's back face to the C-frame's outboard plate (5 × M2.5 × 8). Push the stub axle into the front-wall bearing.
12. **Tension:** slide the idler outward until the belt is tight (≈ 2–3 mm deflection under a firm finger), then lock the M3 nut. Tighten the 20T grub screws on the filed flat (threadlocker).
13. **Snowshoe:** slide the shank's end plug between the snowshoe's cheeks (between the two lips), push the **M3 × 25** through cheek – plug – cheek, add an **M3 wave washer** and the **nylock**. Tighten until the washer is just flat, then back off a quarter turn: the pad must hold its angle when you swing the leg by hand but rotate easily when you press it flat on the bench. Check it stops on both lips.

## Cable routing
The hip and knee motor leads leave the back faces (Ø20 holes in both module plates). Run them along the top of the C-frame column, then down the inboard plate to the ab/ad axis. Zip-tie them at the module back flange, leave a 60 mm service loop around the ab/ad axis, and zip-tie them to the spine slots (`body_spine` has two slots). Check at q0 = ±45° that nothing is stretched or pinched. The ab/ad motor's own leads stay on the fixed bracket.

## Hard stops
- Hip: the C-frame column (`module_frame_outboard`) stops the thigh at ±107°. Firmware limit ±100°.
- Knee: firmware only (mechanically free −30° … 163°); the CAD limit mate stops at 150°. TODO: stop lug.
- Ab/ad: firmware ±45° (mechanically free ±98°). TODO: spine stop lugs.
